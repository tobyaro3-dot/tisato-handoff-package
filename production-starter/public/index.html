About Us

Transportation built around reliability, dignity, and medical routines.

TISATO Transportation Services INC is a Central Florida non-emergency medical transportation company supporting passengers, families, healthcare facilities, and care coordinators.

We specialize in reliable recurring transportation for dialysis patients, seniors, wheelchair users, and individuals with limited mobility or chronic health conditions. With door-through-door assistance and thoughtful communication, every ride is treated as an opportunity to protect, support, and dignify the people we serve.

Our values are safety, compassion, reliability, integrity, and excellence. We believe transportation should never stand between a person and their care.
