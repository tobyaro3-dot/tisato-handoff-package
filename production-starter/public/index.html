About Us

Transportation built around reliability, dignity, and medical routines.

TISATO Transportation Services INC is a Central Florida non-emergency medical transportation company supporting passengers, families, healthcare facilities, and care coordinators.

We specialize in reliable recurring transportation for dialysis patients, seniors, wheelchair users, and individuals with limited mobility or chronic health conditions. With door-through-door assistance and thoughtful communication, every ride is treated as an opportunity to protect, support, and dignify the people we serve.

Our values are safety, compassion, reliability, integrity, and excellence. We believe transportation should never stand between a person and their care.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

1. What is Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT)?

NEMT helps passengers get to healthcare appointments when they need reliable transportation, mobility support, or care-focused trip coordination.

2. How do I schedule a ride with TISATO?

Submit the online request form or call dispatch at (844) 884-7286. A team member will review your ride details and follow up.

3. Are TISATO rides safe and regulated?

TISATO focuses on safe, professional, mobility-aware transportation with trained drivers, dispatch review, and documented trip details.

4. Does insurance cover NEMT services?

Coverage depends on your plan and benefits. Contact TISATO so we can help review the trip request and discuss available options.
