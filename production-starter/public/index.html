FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

NEMT is safe and reliable transportation for people who need help getting to medical appointments, therapy sessions, treatments, or other health-related visits. Unlike regular ride-share services, NEMT is designed for seniors, people with mobility challenges, wheelchair users, and patients who require extra care.

Scheduling a ride is simple. Call, book online, or text us directly to speak with a team member who will gather your trip details, including pickup location, appointment time, and mobility needs. You can book one-time or recurring rides, and once confirmed, you can rely on safe, punctual, and compassionate service every time.

Yes. Our vehicles are inspected regularly, our drivers are background-checked and CPR-trained, and all wheelchair and mobility equipment is secured using industry-standard safety systems. We follow strict state and federal guidelines to ensure every ride meets a high level of safety and care.

Many insurance plans may cover non-emergency medical transportation depending on your benefits. Some Medicaid plans include NEMT coverage. If your insurance does not cover transportation, we also offer affordable private-pay rates, and our team can help you understand your options.

About Us

Transportation built around reliability, dignity, and medical routines.

TISATO Transportation Services INC is a dialysis-focused non-emergency medical transportation provider serving Winter Garden, Clermont, Orlando, and surrounding Central Florida communities. As a locally owned and family-operated business, we were created to ensure that transportation is never a barrier to care.

We specialize in reliable, recurring wheelchair transportation for dialysis patients, seniors, and individuals with limited mobility or chronic health conditions. With ADA-compliant vehicles, securement systems, and door-to-door assistance, riders receive safe, on-time service to and from dialysis centers, medical appointments, rehab facilities, and outpatient clinics.

What sets TISATO apart is our relationship-based approach. Riders are not just trips on a schedule; they are people whose routines, needs, and stories matter. As an owner-operated company, we offer direct communication, flexible coordination with facilities, and personal accountability that larger brokers and random vans cannot match.

Our core values are safety, compassion, reliability, integrity, and excellence. These values guide every ride we provide and every decision we make.
