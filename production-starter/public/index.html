About Us

Transportation built around reliability, dignity, and medical routines.

TISATO Transportation Services INC is a private non-emergency medical transportation provider serving Winter Garden, Clermont, Orlando, Ocoee, Windermere, Kissimmee, Davenport, and surrounding Central Florida communities. As a locally owned, family-operated business, our mission is to ensure that transportation is never a barrier to accessing quality healthcare.

We specialize in safe, reliable transportation for seniors, wheelchair users, and individuals with limited mobility traveling to medical appointments, dialysis treatments, rehabilitation centers, hospitals, outpatient clinics, and other healthcare destinations. With ADA-compliant vehicles, secure wheelchair restraint systems, and door-to-door assistance, every ride is designed to provide comfort, dignity, and peace of mind. Whether it's a one-time appointment or recurring transportation, TISATO proudly serves individuals, families, assisted living communities, skilled nursing facilities, rehabilitation centers, and healthcare providers throughout Central Florida.

What sets TISATO apart is our relationship-based approach. Every passenger is more than a scheduled ride, they are a person whose needs, routines, and well-being matter. As an owner-operated company, we provide direct communication, flexible coordination with healthcare facilities, and personal accountability that larger transportation providers and broker-based services often cannot match.

Our core values are Safety, Reliability, Compassion, Integrity, and Excellence. These values guide every ride we provide and every decision we make.
