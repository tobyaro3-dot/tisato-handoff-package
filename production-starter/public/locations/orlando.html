Orlando Medical Transportation

Private Medical Transportation in Orlando

TISATO provides private-pay non-emergency medical transportation in Orlando for riders who need a calm, dependable way to get to appointments, care facilities, and health-related visits.

Services available in Orlando

Families and care coordinators can request wheelchair transportation, ambulatory transportation, dialysis transportation, hospital discharge transportation, recurring appointment rides, and long-distance medical transportation reviewed by request.

Why choose TISATO

Our team focuses on clear communication, respectful support, and a private transportation experience that feels organized from pickup through drop-off.

Local transportation information

Orlando riders often need transportation to medical appointments, outpatient visits, therapy, dialysis, and discharge pickups. We also help with trips that connect Orlando with Winter Garden, Ocoee, Windermere, Kissimmee, Clermont, Davenport, and other Central Florida communities.

Orlando FAQs

Do you provide transportation in Orlando?

Yes. TISATO provides private-pay non-emergency medical transportation throughout Orlando and nearby Central Florida areas.

Can you transport riders to Orlando medical campuses?

Yes. We can review trips to hospitals, clinics, therapy offices, dialysis centers, and other non-emergency medical destinations in Orlando.

Do you provide dialysis transportation in Orlando?

Yes. Dialysis transportation can be requested for one-time or recurring schedules.

Can family members ride along?

Companion requests can be shared during booking so our team can review the trip details.

How do I schedule transportation?

Use the ride request form or call (844) 884-7286 with the pickup address, destination, appointment time, and mobility needs.
