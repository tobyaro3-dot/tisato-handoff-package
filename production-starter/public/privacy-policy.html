SMS Communications

By providing your mobile phone number and opting in to receive text messages from TISATO Transportation Services, you consent to receive SMS communications related to transportation scheduling, appointment reminders, service updates, customer support, and other business-related notifications.

Message frequency may vary depending on your transportation needs, appointments, and interactions with our services. Recurring messages may be sent.

TISATO Transportation Services respects your privacy. Your personal information and mobile phone number will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any text message you receive from us. After opting out, you will no longer receive SMS messages unless you opt back in.

For additional assistance or more information, reply HELP to any message or contact us directly through our website.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and service plan.
