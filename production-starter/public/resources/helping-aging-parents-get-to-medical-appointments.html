Helping Aging Parents Get to Medical Appointments

When a parent needs help getting to appointments, transportation should feel clear, respectful, and easy to coordinate.

Gather the ride details

Write down the pickup address, appointment address, appointment time, return-trip needs, and the best phone number for day-of communication.

Talk through mobility needs

Ask whether your parent uses a wheelchair, walker, cane, or needs extra time getting ready. Clear details help the ride provider prepare.

Consider recurring appointments

If appointments happen weekly or monthly, recurring transportation may reduce planning stress for the family.

Related services

Explore ambulatory transportation, wheelchair transportation, and recurring medical transportation.
