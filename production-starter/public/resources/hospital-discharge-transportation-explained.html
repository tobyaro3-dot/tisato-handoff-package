Hospital Discharge Transportation Explained

Hospital discharge transportation helps a rider leave a facility and travel to a home, residence, or other non-emergency destination.

What information is helpful?

Families should have the facility name, pickup instructions, destination address, best contact number, timing, and mobility details ready.

Why timing matters

Discharge timing can change. Clear communication helps the transportation team review availability and plan the pickup with less confusion.

Know the rider's mobility needs

Share whether the rider is ambulatory, uses a wheelchair, or needs extra time and support getting settled for the ride.
