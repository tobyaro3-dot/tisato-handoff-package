Dialysis Transportation

Preparing for a Dialysis Appointment Ride

Dialysis transportation works best when pickup times, return plans, and mobility details are clear before the ride.

Confirm the appointment details

Have the dialysis center name, appointment time, pickup address, and best contact number ready. If the schedule repeats, share the usual days and times.

Plan the return trip

Some dialysis visits can vary in length. Tell the transportation team whether you need a planned return pickup or a wait-time review.

Share mobility needs

Let the team know if the rider uses a wheelchair, walker, cane, or needs extra time getting to and from the vehicle.
