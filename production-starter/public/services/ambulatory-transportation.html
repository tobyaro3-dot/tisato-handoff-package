Ambulatory Transportation

Ambulatory Transportation in Central Florida

TISATO provides private ambulatory transportation for riders who can walk but still need a dependable, care-aware ride to medical appointments.

Who this service is for

Ambulatory transportation is for riders who do not use a wheelchair but may need extra time, steady communication, or help coordinating pickup and drop-off.

It is often used for seniors, patients attending follow-up appointments, therapy patients, and families who want a more reliable medical transportation option.

How ambulatory transportation works

Submit the pickup location, destination, appointment time, and any mobility notes. Our team reviews the trip, confirms availability, and helps keep the schedule clear.

Rides can be requested for one-time appointments or recurring care routines.

Why families choose TISATO

Families choose TISATO for punctual communication, private-pay simplicity, and a calm transportation experience for non-emergency medical needs.

We serve Central Florida, including Orlando, Winter Garden, Clermont, Ocoee, Windermere, Kissimmee, and Davenport.

Ambulatory transportation FAQs

What does ambulatory transportation mean?

It means transportation for riders who can walk, with or without light assistance, and do not require wheelchair transportation.

Can I use this for therapy appointments?

Yes. Ambulatory rides can be requested for therapy sessions, specialist visits, routine appointments, and other care-related trips.

Can I book a recurring schedule?

Yes. TISATO can review recurring transportation requests for ongoing medical routines.

Do you serve outside Orlando?

Yes. Our primary service area includes several Central Florida communities. Contact us if your city is nearby but not listed.
