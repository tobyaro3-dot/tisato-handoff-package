Hospital Discharge Transportation

Hospital Discharge Transportation in Central Florida

TISATO supports non-emergency hospital discharge trips with private transportation for riders going home, to a care residence, or to another non-emergency destination.

Who this service is for

This service is for riders who need transportation after a hospital discharge and do not require emergency medical transport.

It can support wheelchair riders, ambulatory riders, seniors, and families coordinating a safe return from a care setting.

How hospital discharge transportation works

Provide the facility name, pickup location, discharge timing, destination address, mobility details, and contact phone number. Our team reviews the trip and confirms availability.

Because discharge timing can shift, clear communication helps us coordinate the ride as smoothly as possible.

Why families choose TISATO

Families choose TISATO for calm coordination, private-pay simplicity, and respectful support during a transition that can already feel stressful.

Our service area includes Central Florida, Orlando, Winter Garden, Clermont, Ocoee, Windermere, Kissimmee, and Davenport.

Hospital discharge transportation FAQs

Can TISATO pick up from a hospital?

Yes, when the trip is non-emergency and availability is confirmed.

Can wheelchair riders use this service?

Yes. Include wheelchair details when requesting the ride so we can review the transportation needs.

What information is needed?

We need the facility name, pickup instructions, destination, timing, rider name, contact number, and mobility details.

Can you take the rider home?

Yes. We can review trips from a hospital to a home, residence, or other non-emergency destination.

Do you provide medical treatment during transport?

No. TISATO provides non-emergency transportation and does not provide medical treatment.
